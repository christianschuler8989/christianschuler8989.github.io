Dialect-Aware NLP

Developing NLP solutions for Kurdish dialectal variation - and beyond

Summary

My PhD project: developing NLP solutions tailored to Kurdish dialectal variation, with the long-term goal of extending the methodology to other dialect-rich language families.

Overview

Dialect-Aware NLP is the working title for my PhD research at Saarland University. The core question is: how do we build NLP systems that do not merely tolerate dialectal variation but actively account for it? The project takes Kurdish as its primary testbed - a language family with extensive, well-documented dialectal diversity and very limited digital resources - and develops modular, transferable solutions intended to generalise to other dialect-rich, low-resource language situations.

Why Kurdish?

Kurdish is an ideal research language for dialect-aware NLP for several reasons. It has a large number of speakers across multiple countries, yet is severely underrepresented in NLP benchmarks and tools. Its dialects - Kurmanji, Sorani, Zazaki, Gorani, and many sub-varieties within each - are sometimes mutually unintelligible, yet existing NLP resources conflate them into a single 'Kurdish' label. This makes Kurdish a challenging and scientifically interesting case study, and any improvements directly benefit a real community of speakers.

Research Threads

  • Dialectal Filtering: deriving dialect-specific sub-corpora from large noisy, mixed-dialect text collections.
  • Kurdish Voices: dialect-aware fine-tuning of ASR models for spoken Kurdish varieties.
  • Kurdish Interpretability: using mechanistic interpretability to understand what language models learn about Kurdish dialects.
  • Dialect Mapping: building a comprehensive geographic and typological map of Kurdish dialectal variation.
  • Kobani Analysis: in-depth phonological and morphological study of the Kobani variety as a case study.

Long-Term Vision

The methods developed for Kurdish are designed to be language-agnostic wherever possible. The long-term goal is a transferable methodology for dialect-aware NLP that can be applied to other dialect-rich, low-resource language families - particularly those where dialect variation is linguistically significant but computationally ignored.
